Got questions?

A few things clients ask us most often.

What does Elextric do?

Elextric supplies, installs, repairs and maintains wine conditioning units (WineMaster, EuroCave, TeknoPoint, Friax, Wine Guardian) and specialist refrigeration (Corner Fridge, Norcool), alongside domestic and commercial electrical work across London.

Where is Elextric located?

We're based in London and cover the capital and South East, travelling further afield for specialist wine conditioning and refrigeration work when needed.

How can I contact Elextric?

Call 0203 286 8525, email info@elextric.co.uk, or fill in the enquiry form above and a member of the team will come back to you with pricing and availability.

Are you certified and experienced?

Yes — our engineers are NAPIT, REFCOM and TrustMark accredited, and our lead engineer has worked directly with WineMaster's dealer network since 2015.
