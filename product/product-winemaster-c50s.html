Built for serious cellars

The WineMaster C50S is a through-wall monoblock unit for insulated rooms up to 50 m³. Cooling and heating keep your collection stable year-round.

For many years the Fondis WineMaster C50S has been valued for its adaptability to any properly insulated room. As part of the built-in series it is installed through the wall, using an energy-efficient system and a straightforward wooden frame for mounting.

Two models are available: the C50S (cooling + heating) and the C50SR, which adds anti-frost protection for the compressor when the condenser side is exposed to colder outdoor conditions.

Why clients choose the C50S

  • Designed for cellars up to 50 m³ (subject to insulation)
  • Electronic thermostat, preset at 12 °C, adjustable range
  • Quiet operation — typically 40–45 dB at 1 m
  • Integrated lighting and dust filter
  • Wooden installation frame supplied
  • Heating function for rooms with fluctuating ambient temperatures

Cooling capacity

1200 W at 15 °C — sufficient for well-insulated spaces up to 50 m³.

Temperature control

Preset 12 °C with adjustment typically between 8–18 °C. Stable air temperature protects the wine.

Quiet & reliable

Rubber silent-blocks and reaction turbines keep noise low and efficiency high.

Easy installation

Through-wall design with wooden frame. Our engineers handle the full install and commissioning.
