What to have ready when you call

A clear first conversation saves a wasted visit. We’ll ask for:

  • Make & model (sticker on the unit or old invoice)
  • What’s wrong in plain language (warm, iced, noisy, dead, tripping)
  • Postcode and access notes
  • Photos of the unit and the controller display if you can

Where we can, we load the likely parts before we travel so the first visit is the last visit.
