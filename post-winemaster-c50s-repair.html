Case study · #N26WGD WineMaster C50S On-site repair

Winemaster C50S repaired in just 10 minutes on site

Faulty controller diagnosed and replaced during a single visit — unit back to stable temperatures the same day.

During a recent visit to an existing wine cellar, I was called upon to inspect a unit experiencing issues. The client had reported that their Winemaster C50S was freezing and causing temperatures to drop below 10 degrees.

10min

With extensive knowledge of the WineMaster range, the problem was identified within 10 minutes of arriving. The issue was traced to a faulty controller, which was promptly replaced — restoring full operation the same visit.

If your wine cellar is equipped with a WineMaster and requires servicing or fault diagnosis, contact Elextric Ltd. We offer nationwide support across the UK for any wine cellar utilising a WineMaster conditioning unit.

Elextric Ltd provides repair, installation and upkeep for WineMaster systems. Our team is committed to delivering clear value and ensuring your equipment operates at its best.
